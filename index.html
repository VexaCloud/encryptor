About

Message Encryptor applies a configurable stack of classical encodings and ciphers.

Enable or disable individual layers in Settings. Supply a single alphabetic word as the Vigenère key (or leave blank for the default).

  • Layers are applied in a fixed order on encrypt and reversed on decrypt.
  • “Dead characters” inject random noise at a chosen frequency for extra obfuscation.
  • The same key and settings must be used for both encrypt and decrypt.
